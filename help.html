🚀 Getting started

  1. Open the app from its Home Screen icon (not from Safari). The Home Screen app and Safari keep separate data.
  2. Allow location when it asks.
  3. First time only: tap ⋯ → Import JSON / CSV and choose your route file. Your stops load in delivery order.
  4. Once, with Wi-Fi, scroll the map along your whole route in both Street and Satellite views (layers button, top right). That saves the map for areas without service.
Back up oftenEverything lives only on your phone. Tap ⋯ → Backup + photos at the end of any day you add pins or photos, and save it to Files or iCloud.

📍 Reading the pins

Each stop can have two pins: the mailbox (for DPS, flats and small parcels) and the house (where packages go). A dashed line joins them.

Curbside · Cluster box (CBU) · Door/porch slot · Parcel locker 8Grey = already done today. 14📦Orange ring + 📦 = on today's package list. 18501Dashed orange = house pin was auto-placed and still needs checking. RV12Space label — just a marker, not a stop. Hidden when zoomed out.

Pins and lines get smaller as you zoom out, and turn into small dots when you're zoomed far out, so the map stays readable. Zoom in to see the numbers again. During guidance, the next stop stays big with a pulsing yellow ring.

You can change what the numbers on pins show in ⋯ → Numbers shown on pins.

✏️ Adding & editing stops

Tap any pin or list row to open the stop. Tap Edit to change it.

Delivery order

  • Seq # is the delivery order. Type a number that's already used and the stop is inserted there; everything after moves back one.
  • On a stop's page, ＋ Insert after adds a new stop right behind it, in the same section.
  • Save & next ▶ saves and opens the next stop, so you can work down the route without going back to the list.

Placing pins

  • 📍 GPS here — most accurate. Stand at the box (or the door) and tap it.
  • Tap map — tap the exact spot. Use Satellite view to see the actual box or house.
  • Same as prev stop — copies the mailbox from the stop before (handy for boxes on one post).
  • Look up address — rough guess only; rural addresses are often off.
  • After placing, drag the pin to fine-tune it.

Notes, photos, businesses

  • Notes — dogs, gate codes, where to leave parcels.
  • 📷 Add photo — take one or pick from your library. Tap a thumbnail to see it full screen. Photos are shrunk to save space.
  • This is a business — add the name, days open and hours. The stop then shows Open now / Closed now.
Cluster boxesPlace the mailbox for the first unit in an RV park or CBU, then on that stop's page tap "Use this mailbox for … other stops" to fill in the whole section at once.

⚡ Filling in pins fast

Quick place (best for mailboxes)

  1. ⋯ → 📍 Start quick place.
  2. The panel shows the next stop without a mailbox pin. Pull up to that box and tap 📍 Mailbox here. It saves from GPS and moves to the next one.
  3. 🏠 House here places the house pin for the current stop. ＝ Same box reuses the last box spot. Skip ▶ and ◀ move through stops.
  4. At a cluster box, one tap fills every unit in that section.

Auto-place houses

⋯ → 🏠 Auto-place houses looks up your addresses online (about one per second — keep the app open with service). Only exact house-number matches are placed, and they show dashed orange until you check them. Many rural addresses won't be found.

To confirm one: open the stop, look at it on Satellite view, then tap ✓ House pin is right — or edit and drag it onto the right house.

🏷 RV space numbers

  1. ⋯ → Add / edit space numbers. Zoom in close on the park.
  2. Type the first label (for example RV1), then tap the map on each space. The number goes up by one each time: RV1, RV2, RV3…
  3. Tap a yellow label to rename or delete it, drag to move it, Undo to remove the last one, Done when finished.

Labels are never joined by lines, and they hide when you zoom out so the map stays readable.

🧭 Route guidance

Starting

  • From the beginning or any seq #: ⋯ → Start at seq # → ▶ Start.
  • From a particular stop: open it → 🧭 Guide me from this stop.
  • To continue where you stopped: ⋯ → ▶ Resume at #.

Leaving and rejoining the route

  • If you head away from all your stops, the panel shows ↩ Off route and the voice goes quiet. That time and those miles are logged as off-route.
  • When you come back near any stop that isn't done yet, it picks up from there — even if it's a different point on the route.
  • Early delivery to a stop further along: stop there for a few seconds and it's marked done. Guidance keeps pointing at where you were in the route, and skips that stop later.
  • If you keep delivering in order from that new spot (two stops in a row), it switches to following you from there.
  • Driving past later stops without stopping doesn't mark them done.
  • Anything you never reached shows as missed in the stats.

Street-by-street turns (optional)

Turn on in ⋯ → Street-by-street turns. It needs service and uses a free online route service, so it can be unavailable. Without it you still get the stop-to-stop voice and arrow.

Keep the app on screeniPhone stops giving location to web apps when the screen locks or you switch apps. Set Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never while driving, and come back to the app after using Apple Maps. Set things up while parked, and follow your station's rules about phones in the vehicle.

📦 Packages

Today's packages (from the packing slip)

  1. Tap 📦 at the top of the screen.
  2. Tap the text box, tap it again, and choose Scan Text. Point the camera at the address on the slip and tap Insert. Or just type the house number and street.
  3. Tap Find on my route. A ✓ means it's added. If two stops share the number, pick the right one.

Flagged stops get an orange ring and 📦 on the map. During guidance you'll see "Next stop has a package" and hear "with a package". When you arrive it shows 📦 PACKAGE HERE. You can also flag a stop from its page with ＋ Package today. The list clears itself the next day.

Timing a package delivery

  1. After you arrive, tap 📦 as you leave the vehicle. At a cluster box it asks which unit.
  2. Tap 📦 again when you're back in the vehicle. It says how long it took, and takes the stop off today's list.

While the package timer runs, guidance won't skip ahead even if you walk near other stops.

Package rating

Every package you time — plus any you add by hand with ＋ Log a package on a stop's page — counts toward that address. The busiest address on your route is 10/10 📦; the rest are scaled from it. You'll see the rating in the list and on each stop.

📊 Stats & your path

Recorded automatically whenever guidance is running. Open with ⋯ → 📊 Route stats & charts. Pick a day from the menu at the top.

Slowest dropsWhere you spent the most time stopped, with package time and your usual time there.

🗺 Show my path on the map draws where you drove that day: blue on route, red off route, with a red dot where each detour started. Tap Hide in the banner to clear it.

Export times (CSV) saves every drop's times for a spreadsheet. Stats live only on your phone.

🚐 Vehicle check

  1. ⋯ → 🚐 Vehicle check. Enter the vehicle # and odometer.
  2. Tap ✓ OK, ⚠ Problem or N/A for each item. Mark the rest OK fills in anything left.
  3. For a problem, describe it and tap 📷 Add photo.
  4. Finish & save, then Share report to text or email it, with photos attached.

Your progress saves as you go, so you can leave and come back. Past checks keeps your history.

Use your station's listThe built-in list is a starter list, not the official USPS form. Tap Edit checklist and type your station's items, one per line.

📶 Offline & backups

Works with no service

Your stops, search, notes, photos, GPS pins, quick place, voice guidance, pause/resume, off-route pickup, timing, path recording, packages, stats and the vehicle check.

Needs service

Street-by-street turns, auto-place houses, address lookup, sharing a report, and map areas you've never viewed before.

Backups

  • Backup (no photos) — small file: stops, space labels, times, vehicle checks.
  • Backup + photos — everything, larger.
  • Import JSON / CSV restores a backup. "OK" replaces what's on the phone; "Cancel" adds to it.

Updating the app

  1. Upload the new files to your route-map repo on GitHub with exactly the same names (route-map.html, sw.js, help.html). Watch for "route-map 2.html" — rename it first.
  2. Wait 2 minutes, open the app with service, close it fully, and open it again.

Updating never erases your stops — they're stored on the phone, not in the files.

🛠 Troubleshooting

Stuck on a stopTap ▶|. If you're far from the route it will pick up by itself when you get near a stop.
"GPS signal lost"It's retrying on its own. Make sure the app is on screen and the phone isn't locked.
Arrives too early / lateChange ⋯ → Count as arrived within (50–200 ft). Phone GPS is usually within 15–30 ft.
No voiceCheck 🔊 and the phone's ring/silent switch and volume.
Blank map in placesThat area wasn't viewed with service yet. Your pins still show and GPS still works.
Stops missingMake sure you opened the app from the Home Screen icon, not Safari. Restore from your latest backup if needed.
